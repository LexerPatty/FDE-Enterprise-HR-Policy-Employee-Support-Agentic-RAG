Normal RAG :
AI searches for information and answers.

Agentic RAG :
AI decides how and where to search, can use multiple tools, and can repeat the search until it has enough information to answer.



Real-life example: Company HR

You ask:
"What is the leave policy for employees working from home?"

NORMAL RAG...........:

Question
   ↓
Search HR documents
   ↓
Retrieve relevant chunks
   ↓
LLM
   ↓
Answer


AGENTIC RAG......  :

Question
   ↓
AI Agent
   ↓
"Which documents do I need?"
   ↓
Search HR policy
   ↓
Didn't find enough information
   ↓
Search remote-work policy
   ↓
Compare both documents
   ↓
Maybe search employee handbook
   ↓
LLM
   ↓
Final answer


Agentic RAG = RAG + an AI agent that controls the retrieval process.
